## 3. Provenance Verification Matrix

### 3.1 Primary Coordinate Geodetic Sources
- **Mars Geodetic Reference Frame:**  
  All areographic coordinates cited in OFFWORLD conform to the **IAU 2000 Mars Planetocentric coordinate system**, with longitudes measured positive eastward from the Airy-0 prime meridian, matching the geodetic datum used by NASA PDS, MGS MOLA, and MRO HiRISE.
- **Lunar Geodetic Reference Frame:**  
  All selenographic coordinates conform to the **Mean Earth/Polar Axis (ME) coordinate system**, consistent with Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) and Apollo landing surface journals.

### 3.2 Authenticity Safeguard
Every image, audio file, and spatial polyline referenced in the OFFWORLD archive corresponds to an authoritative NASA mission product with an authentic NASA ID (e.g., PDS volume, Photojournal PIA, or SVS asset). No synthetic or hallucinated datasets are included.
